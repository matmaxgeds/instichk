Fingerwell

A desk puck with a finger-sized hole. Rest a fingertip in the well and lean it against the walls; eight pressure segments read the push and steer the pointer. Press down on the floor to click.

On screen rate control

Wall pressure sets pointer speed, like a joystick. Lean harder, move faster; relax and the pointer stops.

Working spec sketch dimensions

Body
Ø 100 mm puck, 18 mm tall, weighted base, soft-touch shell
Well
Ø 23 mm, 12 mm deep, sized for an index fingertip with ~3.5 mm play to each wall
Wall sensors
8 curved force-sensing segments at 45° spacing; force vector = Σ segment pressure × segment normal
Floor
Domed click switch, ~1.5 N actuation, 0.5 mm travel
Mapping
Rate control with a small dead zone and a quadratic transfer curve for fine control near centre
